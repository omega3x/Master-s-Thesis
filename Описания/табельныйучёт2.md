# Timekeeping (main process)

**Source:** `Диаграммы/табельныйучёт2.png`  
**Camunda XML:** `XML код/табельныйучёт2.bpmn`  
**Notation:** BPMN (Bizagi Modeler)  
**Participants:** подразделение (department), кадровая служба (HR)

---

## Simple explanation

The department records how much and in what way employees worked (timesheets and sick leave). HR uses that data to build the consolidated timesheet. When both stages are done, HR passes the result to the finance department for calculations and payments.

---

## Detailed description

### Purpose

The diagram describes the end-to-end **timekeeping** business process: from recording working time in a department to handing data over to the finance department.

### Participants (lanes)

| Lane | Role |
|------|------|
| **подразделение** | Organizational unit / department (top lane) |
| **кадровая служба** | HR / personnel department (bottom lane) |

### Elements and flow

**Start (department lane)**  
- **Start event** — green circle on the left in the department lane.

**Task: фиксация рабочего времени** (record working time)  
- Blue **manual / user task** (hand-writing icon).  
- **Related data** (dotted links to documents above the task):  
  - **табели** (timesheets)  
  - **больничные** (sick leave records)

**Split after recording**  
After this task, the flow splits into two paths:

1. **Path A** — stays in the **подразделение** lane and goes horizontally to the **gateway**.  
2. **Path B** — moves down to the **кадровая служба** lane to task **формирование табеля** (timesheet generation).  
   - The **"+"** marker on the task indicates a **sub-process** (details in `табельныйучёт1.md` / `табельныйучёт1.png`).

**Gateway (department lane)**  
- Yellow **diamond** (merge gateway).  
- Receives flows from path A (from recording) and path B (from timesheet generation).

**Task: передача финансовой службе** (transfer to finance)  
- In the **кадровая служба** lane, after the gateway (flow goes right, then down).  
- **User task** (person icon): hand off the result to the **finance department**.

**End**  
- **End event** — red circle on the right in the HR lane.

### Process logic

1. The **department** registers actual working time using timesheets and sick leave data.  
2. **HR** builds the consolidated timesheet (internal sub-process).  
3. When both paths are complete, the flow **merges at the gateway**.  
4. HR **transfers** the result to **finance**, and the process **ends**.

### Link to sub-process

Task **«формирование табеля»** is detailed in diagram `табельныйучёт1.png` (process **«Процесс 1»**): parallel analysis of orders, sick leave, and timesheets, then generation of payroll statements.

**Camunda:** sub-process — `XML код/табельныйучёт1.bpmn` (`Process_TabelnyUchet1`), invoked from the main process `XML код/табельныйучёт2.bpmn` via `callActivity`.
